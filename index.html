Finish What I Started

I am asking for a fair, timely and formally authorized pathway to finish the law degree I began.

I entered law school as a first-generation student with no lawyers in my family. After years of navigating academic, accommodation, human-rights, safety and legal processes, Fall classes are about to begin and I still need a clear, authorized academic decision about how I can return and complete my J.D.

This campaign makes the record understandable, explains why procedural delay can itself become an access-to-justice problem, and gives people concrete ways to help.

The circular access problem

When every process points somewhere else

Academic problem→Human-rights / accommodation issue→Internal office→Petition / appeal→Deadline / good-standing barrier→External route→No immediate academic remedy→Back to academic status

I kept being directed to processes that depended on an unresolved academic status, while the routes identified to fix that status were late, advisory, conditional, or unable to solve the immediate return problem. This is why I am asking for one current, authorized academic determination.

See the full explanation
What I am asking for

A precise, workable resolution

  1. Formal restoration or reinstatement to active J.D. status.
  2. Formal Fall 2026 registration and authorization to earn law-school credit.
  3. Written reconciliation of the earlier withdrawal decision/status with the current academic status.
  4. A practical academic plan to complete the remaining J.D. requirements.
  5. Appropriate accommodations, communication boundaries and implementation steps.
  6. A process timely enough to matter and not endlessly redirect the student to another route.
Professional future

The goal is completion, not conflict.

The campaign is about preserving a path to finish the degree, return to professional training, and continue building a future in law and public service.

Boundary
This campaign does not ask supporters to contact or target individual Western employees. Support should remain focused on fair process, verification, connection and constructive resolution.
