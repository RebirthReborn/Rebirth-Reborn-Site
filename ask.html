The Ask

What I am asking for

A narrow, practical and formally authorized pathway to complete the J.D.

  1. Formal restoration or reinstatement to active J.D. status.
  2. Formal Fall 2026 registration and authorization to earn law-school credit.
  3. Written reconciliation of the earlier withdrawal decision/status with the current academic status.
  4. A practical academic plan to complete the remaining J.D. requirements.
  5. Appropriate accommodations, communication boundaries and implementation steps.
  6. A process timely enough to matter and not endlessly redirect the student to another route.
